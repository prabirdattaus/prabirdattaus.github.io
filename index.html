About me

A life shaped by expertise and curiosity.

Hello, I’m Prabir, an Estate and Trust Practitioner (TEP) based in Toronto. I am also a Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (FCA).

Beyond my professional life, I am a passionate philatelist and traveller. My collecting interests also include coins, currency notes, and matchbox labels—each offering a unique glimpse into the history and culture of its time.

My philatelic speciality is the stamps and postal history of Nauru, a small Commonwealth island nation pronounced “Now-roo” and known locally as Naoero (“Now-ero”). I have participated in national and international exhibitions, where my collections have earned medals.

Having travelled across six continents and visited more than 40 countries, I created this website as a meeting place for curious philatelists and travel enthusiasts—to explore, exchange ideas, and share discoveries.
